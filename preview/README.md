# Previews

Copies of work in progress, for trying on a phone before it's merged into the real app.
Each one goes in a folder named after its branch (a new address each time, so phones don't show an old copy),
e.g. <https://nooogle.github.io/hiragana/preview/my-branch/>.
They're not linked from anywhere, keep their own separate progress and offline cache (the real app leaves
anything under `/preview/` alone), and get deleted once the work is merged.

Previews at the moment:

- [practise-4-step](https://nooogle.github.io/hiragana/preview/practise-4-step/): Practise in 4 steps (watch, trace, copy, recall), with a rough stroke-order check. The old way is under Settings → Practising writing → Classic.
